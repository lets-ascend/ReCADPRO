## 🎨 Icon Design Features

### Visual Design
- **Size**: 48x48px (touch-friendly)
- **Style**: Simple, geometric shapes
- **Color**: Gray (#666666) when unchecked, Blue (#2196F3) when checked
- **Background**: Transparent when unchecked, Light blue (#e3f2fd) when checked
- **Border**: Light gray when unchecked, Blue when checked
- **Rounded Corners**: 4px radius

### E-paper Optimization
- High contrast for visibility
- Simple shapes (no gradients)
- Clear visual distinction between states
- Bold strokes (2px line width)
